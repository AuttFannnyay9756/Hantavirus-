2026 Cruise Ship Outbreak

  • The Event: A cluster of respiratory illness was reported on the Dutch-flagged ship MV Hondius in May 2026.
  • Current Status: Passengers from 19 nationalities have been evacuated and are being monitored during an 8-week incubation period.
  • Containment: Repatriated passengers are isolating to ensure no further spread.

Why This is Not COVID-19

Health experts, including the WHO chief, have confirmed this situation is not a repeat of the COVID-19 pandemic.

  • Transmission Speed: Unlike COVID-19, hantavirus is not a fast-spreading virus.
  • Mode of Spread: It primarily moves through rodent waste (urine, saliva, droppings), not casual airborne contact.
  • Human Contact: Human-to-human transmission is rare and requires very close, prolonged contact.
  • Global Risk: The WHO assesses the risk to the general global population as low.
